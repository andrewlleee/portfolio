Overview

Turning a good club into a visible one.

MAISS (Management Information Student Society) is one of UC Irvine's business student organizations. I joined as a Marketing Intern and grew into Vice President of Marketing, taking on increasing ownership of how the org showed up publicly — from visual identity to the full content calendar behind every event and campaign.

Context

An established club, ready to grow.

MAISS already had a real presence and an active membership base — the opportunity wasn't building something from nothing, it was sharpening a visual identity and content approach that could scale with the org's ambitions. Posts were inconsistent in look and cadence, which meant the brand wasn't compounding the way it could have been.

Brand Refresh Visual Consistency
Process

Rebrand as infrastructure, not a one-off.

I designed a new logo and custom mascot as the foundation, then rebuilt our Figma workflow around reusable templates — so every flyer, story, and post shared the same visual language instead of looking like scattered one-offs. That system was the actual growth lever: it meant every piece of content reinforced the last one, and let us produce at a much higher, more consistent cadence without sacrificing quality.

Design Systems Figma Workflow
Execution

A sustained content operation.

With the system in place, I ran campaigns for Kickoff, FAANG Focus, the Mentorship program, and UCI's Anteater Involvement Fair — producing roughly 2 to 3 new graphics a week across the year, over 50 total. Each campaign was built around the specific event's goals, whether that was recruitment, attendance, or awareness, while staying visually consistent with the broader MAISS identity.

Content Calendar Campaign Planning
Outcome

Real growth, backed by numbers.

The rebrand and content cadence drove a 38% increase in followers in a single week and a 90%+ increase in engagement — measurable proof that a stronger, more consistent visual identity translated directly into a larger, more engaged audience for the org.
