Overview

A live concert in the heart of campus.

Aldrich Park After Dark is a semi-annual live concert series set in UCI's iconic Aldrich Park. As Chief of Staff, I directly oversaw full-cycle planning and execution for the event, including artist logistics for performers San Pacho and JID — managing two commissioners and four interns to deliver a high-energy experience for the campus community, all while running concurrently with UCI's Screen on the Green during Welcome Week.

Context

Two flagship events, one Welcome Week.

As Chief of Staff, I was responsible for UCI's largest Welcome Week programming — which meant planning and executing Aldrich Park After Dark and Screen on the Green concurrently, not sequentially. That meant two separate vendor timelines, two production crews, and two sets of artist or licensing logistics, all converging in the same few weeks with no room for either event to slip.

Concurrent Planning Cross-Team Coordination
Execution

From negotiation to night-of.

I led negotiations to bring San Pacho and JID's performance costs within a $266K budget, then managed two commissioners and four interns across pre-event logistics, promotional oversight, stage and sound setup, and staffing coordination. On the night of the show, that meant being the point person for real-time problem-solving — the kind of on-the-ground judgment calls that don't show up in a planning doc but decide whether a concert actually runs on time.

Artist Negotiation Live Production
Outcome

The smoothest execution yet.

Both events ran on schedule and within budget, and the concurrent Welcome Week programming was recognized by ProStaff as the smoothest execution in the office's history — a direct result of running two full-scale productions in parallel without either one absorbing the other's problems.
