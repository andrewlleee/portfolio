Overview

Fund what you fight for.

Corporations spent over $16.4 BILLION to influence the last election. Shoppyist maps that flow of influence because your shopping cart has become your ballot box.

Shoppyist is a values-based shopping platform that helps people discover brands aligned with their preferences, while surfacing cashback opportunities and community content. I led the UI/UX design process through initial concept, wifreframing, and high-fidelity prototyping.

UI Design UX Research Prototyping Figma
Design Process

Designing for clarity and intent.

The design process began with identifying pain points in the conscious shopping space — users struggled to find reliable, values-based product information in one place. I translated those insights into a clean, intuitive interface that surfaces the most relevant information without overwhelming the user.

Key design decisions included a card-based browsing system, a streamlined values filter, and a minimal color palette that kept the focus on the products themselves.
